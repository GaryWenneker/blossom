# music-release-rights-review

## Workflow
1. Confirm the original composition, source inspiration, creator/performer, lyrics, master file ownership, model/provider license and sound-recording provenance.
2. Check any sampled audio, artist identity, vocal likeness, licensed artwork and credit obligations. The absence of obvious copying is not legal clearance.
3. Verify audio metadata, duration, loudness, cover resolution and distributor-specific requirements using current official docs, not guesses.
4. Separate internal approved-ready status from an actual distributor submission receipt and live streaming availability.
5. Preserve human approval step before publishing; do not infer Spotify streams, revenue, playlist placement or legal rights from generated music.
6. Record approval evidence and next action in the project, and explicitly state which tasks still require a real distributor/provider integration.
